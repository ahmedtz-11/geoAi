How to read it

  • In Sabins and Alteration composites, red/magenta = clay (B11/B12), green/yellow = iron oxide (B4/B2). Yellow-white areas have both: the strongest alteration signal.
  • In Kaufmann, vegetation is green and altered rock pink to red.
  • Use Custom to build any combination.
